What actually
matters here.

A marketing degree, agency experience, real estate knowledge, a portfolio of ads — none of that decides anything. These five do.

  • You write well in Marathi, Hindi and English. Our buyers are Marathi-speaking families. Copy that only works in English is half a job here.
  • You've researched something properly. A thesis, a report, a market study, a long article — work where you had to find the answer yourself.
  • You've written and finished things. Not one draft in a notes app. Pieces you completed and someone else read.
  • You read a lot, by choice. If you can't name what you're reading right now, this isn't your role.
  • You're curious about why people behave the way they do. Buying a home is emotional. You need to be interested in that, not just in words.
The role

What the work
looks like.

Even in month one this is more than writing lines. Roughly half of it is research and thinking — because a hook that hasn't been researched is just a guess that sounds confident.

01 — Research

Understand the buyer before you write a word

  • Study a project, its location, its competitors and its market in depth
  • Work out who buys, why they buy, and what stops them
  • Listen to real sales calls and site-visit feedback to find genuine objections
02 — Writing

Write the lines that have to stop a scroll

  • Ad copy and hooks in Marathi, Hindi and English
  • Scripts for model-led videos and short-form content
  • Headlines for image ads, where every word has to earn its place
03 — Direction

Get it made the way you imagined it

  • Brief the videographer, the model and the editor clearly enough that nobody guesses
  • Sit on shoots and see your script survive contact with reality
  • Build your own swipe file of work worth learning from
04 — Feedback loop

Read the data, then make the next one better

  • Sit with the performance team and understand what actually happened
  • Use it to sharpen the next set of creatives, not to justify the last one

You'll work alongside two senior Creative Strategists and the founder from day one. Nothing here is figure-it-out-yourself.

This has worked before

Two wrong degrees.

Physics research→ Copywriter→ Creative Strategist

Joined with a research background and no marketing experience. The habit of researching properly before forming a view turned out to be the whole job. Now one of the strongest strategists on the team.

Engineering→ Copywriter→ Creative Strategist

Came in curious about content and marketing, started on copy, learned the system from the ground up, and now runs research, writing and client conversations on live projects.

We stopped looking for experienced creative strategists in Pune. That profile barely exists here, and when it does, it usually arrives with habits we'd have to undo. So we hire copywriters with a research habit and build the strategist ourselves.
