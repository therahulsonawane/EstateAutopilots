Day in the life

What your week looks like.

1. Micro-Market Research

You study the locality — Balewadi, Punawale, Kharadi, Kothrud. What are other builders promising? What are buyers frustrated with?

2. Hook & Angle Brainstorms

Writing 15–20 angles for a single campaign. Trilingual: finding the exact Marathi colloquial phrase or Hindi cultural nuance that resonates.

3. Scripting & Collaborating

Writing short-form video scripts and handing them to our video editors and designers with clear visual cues and pacing notes.

4. Looking at the Numbers

Not just writing — seeing which hook generated 40 site visits and which one bombed. Direct feedback from actual sales data.

In-office at our Kothrud, Pune workspace (near Kothrud Depot / McDonald's). Creative strategy moves fast — bouncing hooks across desks with video editors and media buyers happens ten times faster in person.
