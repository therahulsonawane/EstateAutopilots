TASK 01 / CAMPAIGN DIAGNOSIS

01

THE SITUATION

You have launched a Meta campaign for a real estate project. After a few days, the client says:

“Lead quality is poor. Many enquiries are outside the project location and most of them are junk. Please fix the campaign.”
THE PROJECT. THE ENQUIRIES. THE GAP.

Your task

Assume you are the Growth Strategist responsible for this account.

There is no need to write a long theoretical answer. We want to understand the order in which you think.

  1. What would you check first before making any changes?
  2. What changes would you consider at the campaign/ad-set level?
  3. Would you make any changes to targeting, placements, audience structure or optimization? Why?
  4. Is there anything outside the campaign settings that you would check before concluding that targeting is the problem?
  5. What data would you monitor after making the changes to decide whether they worked?
