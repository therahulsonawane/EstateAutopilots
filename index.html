The role

This is not a
copywriting job.

The Creative Strategist decides what we say, who we say it to, and why it will work — before a single rupee of ad spend or a single day of video shoot. It's roughly 60% research and thinking, 40% writing. Here's the actual work.

01 — Research

Understand the buyer better than the builder does

  • Study a project, its location, its competitors and its market in depth
  • Work out who buys, why they buy, and what stops them
  • Listen to real sales calls and site-visit feedback to find genuine objections
02 — Strategy

Decide the message before anyone makes anything

  • Build buyer personas and choose which one each campaign targets
  • Define the project's positioning — the few reasons a buyer should choose it
  • Align the client's sales team so they say the same thing our marketing does
03 — Writing & direction

Turn the thinking into something people watch

  • Write hooks, ad concepts and video scripts
  • Brief the videographer, the model and the editor clearly enough that nobody guesses
  • Build your own swipe file of work worth learning from
04 — Feedback loop

Read the data, then make the next one better

  • Sit with the performance team and understand what actually happened
  • Use it to sharpen the next set of creatives, not to justify the last one

You will be trained into this over roughly eight to nine months, working directly with senior strategists and the founder. You are not doing this alone on day one.
