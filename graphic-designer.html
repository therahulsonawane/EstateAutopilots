Brand Alignment™

You'll never design from a blank page here.

By the time a project reaches us, the developer has already invested in a name, a logo, a brochure and a palette. We don't redesign any of it.

We pull the system out of it — palette, type hierarchy, spacing logic, photographic treatment — and extend that system across every ad, story and social asset until the whole project looks like it came from one mind.

If you can look at a logo and a brochure and say what the digital palette should be, and just as importantly what to leave out, that's the part of this job that matters most.

Fit

What we're actually looking at.

This matters
  • A design or animation degree. B.Des, Communication Design, Applied Arts, BFA, or a full animation programme. This is a priority for us, not a formality.
  • 2–3 years in an agency, studio or in-house team
  • Illustrator and Photoshop at expert level
  • Typography you can defend — scale, tracking, hierarchy
  • Colour as a system, not a mood board. You can build a palette from an existing brand mark.
  • Comfort inside someone else's identity rather than inventing a new one each time
  • A campaign set in your portfolio — ten pieces that hold together beat one beautiful poster
  • Restraint. Negative space before effects.
This doesn't
  • Real estate experience
  • Five to eight years on the CV
  • InDesign or print production
  • A cover letter
  • Awards, certifications, references
  • A long, detailed CV

On the degree: we've found that designers who studied design or animation formally arrive with a clarity about brand identity, typography and colour that's difficult to build later. MIT ID, Symbiosis, ISDI, Abhinav Kala, Bharati Vidyapeeth, MAAC, Arena — all of it counts. It also tells us design is the long game for you, not a stop on the way to something else.
