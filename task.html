03

Keep these four things right

  • Logo and contact number sit at the end of the layout. Nowhere else, and nothing else down there with them.
  • RERA number in the top-right corner. Present and legible, not fighting the headline.
  • Contact number: 89567 93475 / 7030932345
  • Copy stays exactly as given. No rewrites, no additions.
04

Put it in one folder

  • Two JPGs at 1080 × 1920
  • Your editable source file — .ai, .psd, or a Figma link
  • Half a page of reasoning: your palette and what it's doing psychologically, your type choices, what you took from Basil's existing identity and what you deliberately left out

Set the folder to public, then send us the link on WhatsApp.

The half page matters as much as the ads. Anyone can make one good-looking frame — the reasoning is what tells us whether you can sit in a room with a developer and explain a decision.
