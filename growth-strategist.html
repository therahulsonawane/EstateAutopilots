The task

Build the campaign.
Then tell us why.

One real project. One Meta Ads build. Around 90 minutes. Do this before you fill in anything below — the form asks for the link at the end.

Step 0 · Open the project brief Project folder → Everything you need is inside. Read it before you touch Ads Manager.
  1. Build the campaign in your own Ads Manager. Objective, budget, structure — your call. Leave it in draft. Do not publish. Do not spend.
  2. Two ad sets. Define the targeting yourself, based on the project and the buyer personas you think it should reach.
  3. Write the copy. Primary text and headline for each ad set.
  4. Set up the lead form. Fields, questions, intro screen, thank-you screen.
  5. Skip the creative. No images or video needed — we're only looking at the technical build and the thinking.
  6. Screenshot everything and paste it into a Google Doc.
  7. Explain every choice. Under each screenshot, write why. Why that objective, that budget, that audience, that copy angle, those form fields. The reasoning is what we're actually reading.
  8. Answer the six questions below at the end of the same Doc. Short answers are fine.
Answer these in the Doc
  1. CBO or ABO for this project — and what made you choose it?
  2. Advantage+ audience or manual detailed targeting here? What's the trade-off?
  3. A buyer takes three to six months to decide on a flat. How would you structure retargeting across that window?
  4. Instant lead form or a landing page for this project? Defend the choice.
  5. What breaks the learning phase, and how would you avoid it on this budget?
  6. Cost per lead is dropping but site visits aren't moving. Where do you look first, and in what order?
