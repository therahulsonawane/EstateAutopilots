What to write in your Google Doc:

1. Three distinct buyer personas

Define 3 distinct realistic buyers for this Pune project (e.g. IT manager upgrading from 2 BHK, parent looking for school proximity, Pune native looking for balcony space). Include their real fears, hesitations, and financial trigger points.

2. Three video ad hooks (First 3 seconds)

Write 3 scroll-stopping video hooks — one tailored for each persona. Include at least one hook in conversational Marathi and one in Hindi/English. Under each, explain why this hook will work.

3. Three static ad headlines + 1 WhatsApp message

Write 3 high-converting ad headlines for image creatives, plus one 60-word WhatsApp nurture message sent to a buyer who inquired 3 days ago but hasn't booked a site visit yet.

4. Your reasoning & strategy breakdown

Explain what micro-market insights led you to these angles. A rough line with sharp consumer insight beats a pretty line with zero strategy.

Evaluation Rubric

What we're actually looking at.

Depth of research

Did you understand the locality, commute bottlenecks, competing projects in the area, and actual price bands?

Authentic language range

Does your Marathi read naturally like Pune spoken Marathi, or like a robotic English translation? We notice the difference immediately.

Sharp buyer empathy

Do your personas feel like real people with EMIs and school commutes, or generic marketing textbook archetypes?

Strategic reasoning

Can you defend why each line was written? We hire strategists who write, not just writers who fill space.
