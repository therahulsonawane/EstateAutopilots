What to write in your Google Doc

Three things.
In this order.

Read the folder, research the project and the locality on your own, and then write these three sections into one document.

  • Part 01

    Select three personas

    Study the project, its location, its price band and the competing projects around it. Then tell us which three buyer personas you'd target for this project, and why those three.

  • Part 02

    Write the video script

    A complete script for a model-led video ad, aimed at one of the personas you picked. 60 to 90 seconds — roughly 150 to 220 words at a natural speaking pace. Write the whole thing, not an outline.

    • Hook — the first 15 seconds. The opening 15 to 20 words have to pull in the right persona and push everyone else away
    • Body — what holds attention and builds interest
    • Close — what you want the viewer to do, and why now
    • Mark rough timings if you like (0:00–0:15, 0:15–0:50 and so on)

    Write the script in both languages

    • Hinglish — the way people actually speak, not formal Hindi
    • Marathi — written in Marathi, not translated from English
  • Part 03

    Write the image ad content

    One or two image ads for the same persona. Two is better if you have a second angle worth showing. Each ad needs four elements.

    • Headline — short, sharp, works on its own while someone is scrolling
    • Subheadline — one line that supports or sharpens the headline
    • Three bullet points — the reasons that carry the weight
    • A short call to action if you think it needs one
    Shape to follow
    HEADLINE Subheadline — Bullet one — Bullet two — Bullet three Call to action

    And the reasoning. Why this headline over the other five you considered? Why these three bullets and not others?

The reasoning is the part we read first.

Polished lines with no thinking behind them tell us nothing. A rough line with a sharp insight gets you an interview. We can teach the writing — we're looking for how you got there.

How we read it

What we're
actually looking at.

So you know where to spend your time.

Depth of research

Did you go past the folder? Do you know the locality, the competing projects, the price band, who actually lives there?

Quality of reasoning

Can you explain why, in a way that holds up? A weak line with a strong reason beats a clever line with none.

Understanding of the buyer

Does your persona feel like a real person with real hesitations, or like a category copied off a template?

Language range

If you write in Marathi, does it read like it was written in Marathi — or like an English line put through a translator? We can tell.

A few practical things

Before you start.

  • Write it in a Google Doc. Set sharing to “Anyone with the link can view”, then send us the link. If we can't open it, we can't read it.
  • Take two or three days. No deadline and no rush, but don't send something written in twenty minutes — it shows immediately.
  • Use AI if you want, but be ready to defend it. We don't mind the tool. We do mind if you can't explain your own reasoning in the room. That conversation happens in person.
  • Don't try to sound like an ad agency. Plain, clear thinking reads far better here than borrowed jargon.
  • Stuck on something? WhatsApp us. Asking a good question is not a mark against you.
